How it works

Send transactional email from your app in three steps.

No mail servers, no SMTP settings, no vendor dashboards to learn. Get a key, verify your domain, and make one HTTPS call. Two of the three steps we do with you.

01Three steps

11 email

Tell us about your app

Email us and say what you're building. We create a project for you and send you an API key.

2Usually minutes

Verify your domain

We give you the exact DNS records, already formatted for Namecheap, Cloudflare or GoDaddy. Paste them in and your mail is signed and authenticated (DKIM, SPF and DMARC) as soon as DNS catches up.

31 API call

Send

One POST /v1/emails with plain JSON. Copy the ready-made helper for Node.js, Python or Go from the docs.

02Developer experience

The API you'd design if you were in a hurry and had good taste.

One resource, predictable JSON and error codes you can switch on. The docs give you a complete, copy-paste client for your language, with retries and idempotency already wired in.

  • Errors that tell you what to fix: sender_domain_not_allowed, invalid_recipient with the bad addresses listed, attachments_too_large, and more.
  • Works from any backend: Node.js, Python, Go, or anything that can make an HTTPS request.
from pigeon import api

api("POST", "/v1/emails", {
    "from": "Acme <hello@acme.io>",
    "to": [user.email],
    "template": "welcome",
    "template_data": {"name": user.first_name},
    "idempotency_key": f"welcome-{user.id}",
})
pdf, _ := os.ReadFile("invoice-42.pdf")

email, err := client.Send(ctx, pigeon.SendRequest{
	From:    "Billing <billing@acme.io>",
	To:      []string{"ada@customer.com"},
	Subject: "Your invoice #42",
	Text:    "Hi Ada, your invoice is attached.",
	Attachments: []pigeon.Attachment{
		{Filename: "invoice-42.pdf", Content: pdf},
	},
	IdempotencyKey: "invoice-42-sent",
})
curl -X POST "$PIGEON_URL/v1/emails" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PIGEON_KEY" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "from": "Acme <no-reply@acme.io>",
    "to": ["ada@customer.com"],
    "subject": "Your login code",
